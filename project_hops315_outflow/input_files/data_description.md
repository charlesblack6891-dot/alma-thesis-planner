Target: G205.46-14.56S3 (also known as HOPS 315), an evolved protostar in the Orion
molecular cloud, distance ~400 pc.

ALMA observations: Band 6, project code 2018.1.00302.S (PI: Tie Liu), part of ALMASOP
(the ALMA Survey of Orion Planck Galactic Cold Clumps, a large multi-target survey of
low-mass star formation / pre-stellar cores across many Orion clumps). This target's
specific execution block was publicly released 2020-06-26. Molecular line data for
this source: 12CO (2-1) at 230.53800 GHz and SiO (5-4) at 217.10498 GHz, observed
1 November 2018, calibrated with the standard ALMA pipeline in CASA 6.5.4, robust
weighting factor +2.0.

Angular resolution: synthesized beam approx 0.46" x 0.40" (SiO), approx 0.42" x 0.36"
(CO).

Data products: CO(2-1) and SiO(5-4) line cubes tracing a bipolar molecular jet/outflow
from the protostar.

Science context: this dataset was first analyzed in S. Dutta et al. (2020), and more
recently combined with new JWST/MIRI mid-infrared H2 rotational line imaging (S(7)
through S(1)) in Dutta (2025), ApJ 991, 45, DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/adf8d6, to
characterize the jet's temperature structure. That combined ALMA+JWST study identified
two distinct molecular gas temperature components (warm ~773 K, hot ~2499 K) along the
inner ~800 AU of the jet, estimated a jet mass-loss rate of approx 0.27e-6 Msun/yr (from
the JWST data), and derived a visual extinction of approx 23.3 mag.

Science goal (from the proposal abstract): test whether hypercompact HII regions
around the most massive young stars are structured as ionized accretion disks, as
predicted by cloud-collapse simulations. Prior VLA (7 mm) and ALMA (1.2 mm, H29alpha
line) observations at ~400 AU resolution showed ambiguous ionized structures --
consistent with disks, expanding shells, or bipolar outflows -- limited by continuum
opacity and resolution. This proposal targets the H21alpha line and continuum near
0.45 mm, where the sources become optically thin, at ~100 AU resolution, to
discriminate between these models.

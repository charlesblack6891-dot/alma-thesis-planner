Target: MP Mus (also cataloged as PDS 66), a pre-main-sequence K1 star of mass 1.2 +/- 0.2
Msun and age 3.1 +/- 0.9 Myr, at a Gaia DR3 distance of 97.8 pc. One of the few known
protoplanetary-disk hosts within ~100 pc.

ALMA observations: two combined archival ALMA projects, imaged at two continuum
wavelengths:

- Project code 2021.1.01205.S (PI: Claudio Caceres), Band 7 continuum at 0.89 mm,
  observed 2021-11-20 and 2022-08-19, baselines 41-3638 m.
- Project codes 2017.1.01419.S (PI: Claudio Caceres) and 2017.1.01167.S (PI: Sebastian
  Perez), Band 6 continuum at 1.3 mm, observed 2017-11-16, 2017-12-26, and 2018-01-15,
  baselines 15-8283 m.

Angular resolution / sensitivity: synthesized beam 0.09" x 0.07" at 0.89 mm and
0.12" x 0.10" at 1.3 mm (robust=0.5 weighting); continuum rms approx 4.8e-5 Jy/beam
(Band 7) and 4.1e-5 Jy/beam (Band 6).

Data products: dust continuum images at 0.89 mm and 1.3 mm, a spatially resolved
millimeter spectral-index map (alpha between 0.89-1.3 mm), and gas-disk data showing
the disk extends to ~120 au in gas versus a more compact ~60 au continuum extent.

Science context: this dataset is drawn from a published study (Aguayo et al. 2025,
A&A 698, A165, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202554484) that combined the new Band 7
observations with archival Band 6 data to search for disk substructure. A ring
structure at ~80-85 au had previously been seen in scattered-light near-infrared
imaging, but was not detected in the ALMA Band 6 continuum. This paper reports the
first detection of a ring at ~50 au in the ALMA Band 7 continuum (via continuum
subtraction), measures dust masses of 28.4 +/- 2.8 Mearth (0.89 mm) and
26.3 +/- 2.6 Mearth (1.3 mm), a millimeter spectral index of ~2.2 +/- 0.3, and a
spatial spectral-index gradient from ~1.3 (inner disk) to ~4.0 (outer disk) --
interpreted as evidence for an active radial dust-drift process, though grain growth
is not ruled out as an alternative explanation.
